## Brand & Style

The design system re-imagines long-form curiosity journalism and top-10 trivia into a sharp, credible, and immersive editorial experience. It balances journalistic dignity with the fun, intriguing, and sometimes unsettling nature of listicle-driven discoveries. The target audience comprises trivia enthusiasts, knowledge seekers, and casual readers looking for compelling rabbit holes. 

The aesthetic is **Editorial Minimalism meets Modern Publication**:
- Bright, clinical background whites paired with precise, whisper-thin slate outlines create structured compartments for dense content.
- High-contrast visual architecture pairs confident, structured modern sans-serifs for interface framing and list numbering with comfortable, literary serif typography for long-form reading comfort.
- Distinct color accents drawn from the historic identity (energetic slate blue, terracotta red, and sage green) act as vibrant navigational anchors across varied taxonomy channels without overwhelming text legibility.
- Tactile card micro-interactions and restrained transitions give dignity and longevity to list browsing.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative editorial hierarchy supported by energetic thematic accents. 

### Usage Guidelines
- **Primary (`#39ADC6`):** The signature slate blue. Utilized for global interface actions, primary tabs, reading progress, and prominent list category highlights (e.g., General Knowledge, Science).
- **Secondary (`#D24D33`):** Terracotta red. Reserved for high-urgency badges, featured "Top 10" editorial markers, trending tags, and offbeat categories such as "Weird Stuff" and "Creepy".
- **Tertiary (`#7BB352`):** Crisp sage green. Applied to curation tabs like "Editor's Picks", positive validation, and nature/wildlife taxonomies.
- **Neutral (`#343838`):** Deep charcoal slate. Forms the primary typography color for headlines and crisp UI chrome, paired with `#1E2222` for high-density reading text and `#64748B` for secondary metadata and bylines.
- **Canvas & Borders:** Clean `#FFFFFF` as the base canvas, framed by razor-thin `#E2E8F0` and `#EDF2F7` outlines to structure list columns without visual noise.

## Typography

The typographical pairing produces maximum editorial contrast:
- **Headlines & Interface (`Hanken Grotesk`):** A modern, sharp, clean grotesque sans-serif provides immediate legibility in titles, list numbering, and header navigation. All uppercase category badges rely on `label-tag` with expanded tracking (`0.08em`) to mimic heritage print journalism.
- **Narrative Body (`Newsreader`):** An optical editorial serif designed for uninterrupted on-screen reading. It renders item descriptions, long-form paragraphs, and historical citations with warmth and literary gravity.
- **Ordinal Numbers (`list-rank-number`):** Large numeric indicators (e.g., "10", "9", "8") utilize heavy bold sans-serif styling to maintain list structure clarity across cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The system implements a structured **12-column fixed-max-width editorial grid** (`1280px` maximum viewport width) designed for multi-column content density:
- **Desktop (12 Columns):**
  - **Left Sidebar / Trending Strip:** 3 Columns (`25%` width) for chronological updates, compact thumbnail lists, and editorial author bios.
  - **Central Main Feed:** 6 Columns (`50%` width) dedicated to high-impact primary list features with large 16:9 imagery.
  - **Right Sidebar / Curated Picks:** 3 Columns (`25%` width) for "Editor's Picks", deep-dive series, and topic leaderboards.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Reflows into a 5-column primary feed alongside a 3-column curated drawer; left sidebar collapses into quick horizontal pill filters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Single-column stacked feed. Horizontal scrolling tags header replaces top bar taxonomy.

Vertical rhythm is governed by `space-lg` (24px) between narrative list sections and `space-md` (16px) between list elements.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a clean, crisp, and high-legibility publication environment, the design system avoids heavy shadows, glossy skeuomorphism, and blurred layers.

Visual depth is achieved through **low-contrast outlines, tonal structural surfaces, and subtle hover lifts**:
- **Default Surface:** Flat `#FFFFFF` panels resting on `#FFFFFF` canvas, demarcated by single-pixel `#E2E8F0` hairline rules.
- **Section Headers:** Flat background flood headers (e.g., slate-blue, terracotta-red, or sage-green bars) that ground content modules into clear visual regions.
- **Interactive Card Elevation:** Cards sit flat at resting state with 1px border definition. On hover, they elevate using a light, high-diffusion ambient shadow (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 34, 34, 0.06)`) and a subtle 2px vertical translation (`translateY(-2px)`).
- **Sticky / Header Rails:** Solid background with `border-bottom: 1px solid #E2E8F0`, keeping global navigation anchored without casting heavy drop shadows over editorial copy.

## Shapes

The design system uses a **Soft (Level 1)** shape radius:
- Base interactive elements (buttons, inputs, category tags, notification badges) feature a tight `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- Editorial card containers and featured media thumbnails use `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px) to soften imagery while preserving a disciplined, journalistic grid.
- Structural panels and navigation header bars retain crisp, square outer edges (`0px`) to anchor the publication format.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Brand Slate Blue):** Background `#39ADC6`, white text, 4px border radius, bold typography. Active state darkens to `#2A8FA5`.
- **Accent Action (Terracotta Red):** Background `#D24D33`, white text. Utilized for "Random List" triggers and high-value call-to-actions.
- **Ghost / Editorial Outline:** 1px border `#E2E8F0`, transparent canvas, neutral slate text. Transitions to `#F8FAFB` surface on hover.

### Category Tags & Chips
- **Format:** Micro pill badges styled in uppercase `label-tag` format with `0.08em` tracking.
- **Taxonomy Tints:** 
  - *Weird Stuff:* `#FDF2F0` background with `#D24D33` text.
  - *History & Science:* `#EFF9FB` background with `#39ADC6` text.
  - *Animals & Nature:* `#F3F9EE` background with `#7BB352` text.
  - *Creepy & Unexplained:* `#F5F3FF` background with `#7C3AED` text.

### Editorial Cards
- **Feed Card (Primary Center Column):** 16:9 aspect ratio thumbnail with `rounded-lg` corners, placed above a small category tag chip, bold `headline-md` title, byline in `label-meta`, and a single-sentence teaser in `body-sm`.
- **Compact List Card (Sidebar Column):** 1:1 square micro-thumbnail (64x64px), adjacent title in `headline-sm`, metadata timestamp below, and a prominent right-aligned rank indicator (`10`).
- **Divider:** Subtle hairline rule (`1px solid #EDF2F7`) separating stacked list items.

### Lists & Numbered Entries
- **List Header Item:** A distinct rank badge circle containing the entry countdown number (e.g., `10` through `1`), followed by the headline in `headline-lg`.
- **Entry Body:** Narrative set in `body-md` Newsreader serif with `28px` line-height for optimal reading cadence.

### Form Inputs & Search
- **Search Field:** Crisp white background with a `#E2E8F0` border, `0.25rem` radius, placeholder text in `slate-muted`, expanding smoothly into active state with a `#39ADC6` focused ring.

### Pagination
- Clean numeric pagination: active page highlighted with solid `#D24D33` or `#39ADC6` indicator; next/prev controls styled with clean directional arrows.
